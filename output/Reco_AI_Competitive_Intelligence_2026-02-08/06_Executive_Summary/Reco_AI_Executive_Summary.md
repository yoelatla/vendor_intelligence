## TOP 3 STRATEGIC FINDINGS

### 1. Automated Remediation Gap (CRITICAL -- Revenue & Churn Risk)
Reco operates read-only. This is the **#1 customer complaint** and a direct competitive disadvantage. DoControl, AppOmni, and CrowdStrike Falcon Shield all ship automated remediation today. Without it, Reco loses enterprise evaluations to competitors who close the loop from detection to response. **Action:** Assess build vs. partner in Week 1; ship remediation MVP by end of Q1.

### 2. AI-First Architecture Is a Genuine Differentiator -- Under Threat
Reco's patented Knowledge Graph, first-mover AI Agent Governance, and App Factory (225+ integrations, 10x faster onboarding) are real moats. However, **CrowdStrike's $300M acquisition of Adaptive Shield** creates the most dangerous competitive threat: a platform vendor bundling SSPM into an existing security estate at scale. Reco must extend its AI lead, not just defend it.

### 3. Analyst Visibility Will Determine Enterprise Pipeline
SSPM market is growing from $636M to $900M. Forrester predicts pureplay SSPM acquisitions within 18-24 months. Reco has GigaOm recognition but **lacks Forrester Wave and Gartner MQ/HypeCycle placement** -- both are prerequisites for enterprise shortlists. Zscaler is the only Forrester Leader today. Securing analyst coverage is an urgent go-to-market priority.
